# Privacy Policy for Centaur OS

**Last Updated**: January 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Centaur OS ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
When you use Centaur OS, we collect information that you provide directly:

- **Account Information**: Name, email address, role (Founder/Executive/Apprentice), and workspace details
- **Profile Information**: Bio, contact details (phone, LinkedIn), and professional information
- **Work Data**: OKRs, work plans, tasks, notes, and team assignments
- **Financial Data**: Budget information, burn rate, runway, and cost allocations
- **Team Data**: Team member information, AI tool assignments, and organizational structure

### 1.2 Information We Collect Automatically
- **Usage Data**: Features used, time spent in app, and interaction patterns
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version, and unique device identifiers
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports, error logs, and performance metrics

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Payment Information**: We do not currently process payments or store credit card data
- **Location Data**: We do not track your precise geographic location
- **Contacts**: We do not access your device contacts
- **Camera/Microphone**: We do not access camera or microphone without explicit permission

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- **Provide Core Services**: Enable OKR tracking, team management, work planning, and financial analytics
- **Personalize Experience**: Customize dashboards and recommendations based on your role
- **Improve the App**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and fix bugs
- **Communicate**: Send important updates, notifications, and support responses
- **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
- **Legal Compliance**: Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms of service

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Where Your Data is Stored
- **Local Storage**: Some data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (encrypted)
- **Cloud Storage**: Workspace data is synced to secure cloud servers (if multi-device sync is enabled)
- **Data Centers**: We use industry-standard cloud providers with SOC 2 compliance

### 3.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Access Control**: Role-based permissions ensure users only see authorized data
- **Regular Audits**: We conduct regular security audits and penetration testing
- **Data Backup**: Automatic backups with 30-day retention

### 3.3 Data Retention
- **Active Workspaces**: Data is retained as long as your workspace is active
- **Deleted Workspaces**: Data is deleted within 90 days of workspace deletion
- **Backup Copies**: Backup copies are deleted within 180 days

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We only share data in these limited circumstances:

### 4.1 Within Your Workspace
- Team members in your workspace can see shared work data (OKRs, work plans, team info)
- Permissions are controlled by role (Founder, Executive, Apprentice)

### 4.2 Service Providers
- **Hosting**: Cloud infrastructure providers (AWS, Google Cloud, or similar)
- **Analytics**: Crash reporting and performance monitoring services
- **Support**: Customer support platforms (only when you contact us)

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
- To comply with laws, regulations, legal processes, or government requests
- To protect rights, property, or safety of Centaur OS, users, or the public
- In connection with business transfers (mergers, acquisitions, etc.)

### 4.4 With Your Consent
- Any other sharing will require your explicit consent

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

### 5.1 Access and Correction
- You can view and edit your profile and workspace data within the App
- Contact us to request a copy of your data (data portability)

### 5.2 Data Deletion
- You can delete specific OKRs, work plans, and team members within the App
- Contact us to delete your entire workspace and account
- Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

### 5.3 Opt-Out Rights
- **Marketing**: Opt out of promotional emails (we currently don't send any)
- **Analytics**: Disable crash reporting and analytics in Settings → Privacy
- **Notifications**: Control push notifications in device settings

### 5.4 Account Closure
- You can close your account anytime by contacting us
- All data will be deleted within 90 days of account closure
