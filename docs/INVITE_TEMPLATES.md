## Best Practices

### Do

- Personalize the {Role/Sector} mention
- Reference how you found them (event, referral, LinkedIn)
- Keep it short - under 150 words for email, under 300 characters for LinkedIn
- Follow up once after 5-7 days if no response

### Don't

- Don't send mass automated messages
- Don't promise anything beyond what the platform offers
- Don't store their email unless they provide it via opt-in
- Don't add people to the public marketplace without their consent

## Follow-up Sequence

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 0 | Send initial invite |
| 7 | First follow-up if no response |
| 14 | Mark as "stale" - create follow-up task |
| 21 | Final follow-up or archive |

The system auto-creates follow-up task drafts for stale invites (14+ days, no response).
