# Partnerships Playbook

**Building Your Apprentice & Talent Pipeline Through Strategic Partnerships**

---

## Overview

Partnerships provide scalable, quality candidate pipelines. Focus on:
1. **Universities** - Degree apprentices, final-year students, graduates
2. **Bootcamps** - Career changers, self-taught developers
3. **Community Groups** - Professional networks, alumni associations
4. **Apprenticeship Providers** - Levy-funded training providers

---

## Partner Categories

### Tier 1: Universities (Primary Source)

**Target Departments:**
- Business School (Finance, Operations, Strategy)
- Engineering (CAD, Mechanical, Software)
- Data Science / Analytics
- Economics / Finance

**Key Contacts:**
- Careers Service / Employability Team
- Industrial Placements Coordinator
- Apprenticeship Lead
- Department Heads

**Value Proposition:**
- Real startup experience for students
- Mentorship from experienced fractional executives
- Potential conversion to full-time roles
- Portfolio of work to show employers

### Tier 2: Bootcamps & Training Providers

**Types:**
- Coding bootcamps (General Assembly, Le Wagon, Makers)
- Data science programs (Flatiron, DataCamp)
- Product management courses (Product School)
- Finance certifications (CFA, ACCA prep programs)

**Key Contacts:**
- Careers / Placement Team
- Partnerships Manager
- Alumni Relations

**Value Proposition:**
- Employment outcomes improve their stats
- Real projects for portfolios
- Referral fees potentially available

### Tier 3: Community Groups

**Types:**
- Professional networks (LinkedIn groups, Slack communities)
- Alumni associations
- Sector-specific groups (FinTech Connect, Hardware Club)
- Diversity-focused groups (Code First Girls, Colorintech)

**Key Contacts:**
- Community Managers
- Event Organizers
- Newsletter Editors

**Value Proposition:**
- Quality opportunities for members
- Content/speaker opportunities
- Cross-promotion

### Tier 4: Apprenticeship Providers (UK)

**Levy-Funded Providers:**
- Multiverse
- Whitehat
- BPP
- QA

**Key Contacts:**
- Employer Partnerships Team
- Account Managers

**Value Proposition:**
- Levy-funded training costs covered
- Structured learning pathways
- Quality assurance via Ofsted ratings

## Outreach Strategy

### Phase 1: Research & List Building (Week 1)

1. Create target list of 20-30 partners
2. Categorize by tier and priority
3. Find contact names via LinkedIn
4. Add to Partners directory in CursorOS

**Priority Scoring:**
| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Volume potential | 30% |
| Quality alignment | 30% |
| Ease of access | 20% |
| Strategic value | 20% |

### Phase 2: Initial Outreach (Week 2-3)

1. Send personalized emails (see INVITE_TEMPLATES.md)
2. Connect on LinkedIn
3. Track in Partners directory
4. Create WHAT task drafts for follow-ups

**Status Flow:**
```
not_contacted → contacted → in_conversation → active → paused
```

### Phase 3: Conversion Calls (Week 3-4)

**Call Agenda (30 mins):**
1. Intro + context (5 mins)
2. Their current placement challenges (10 mins)
3. How Fractional Foundry works (10 mins)
4. Next steps (5 mins)

**Key Questions to Ask:**
- How many students/graduates looking for placements per cohort?
- What types of roles are hardest to place?
- What does a successful partnership look like for you?
- Who else should we be talking to?

### Phase 4: Partnership Activation (Ongoing)

**For Active Partners:**
1. Share intake form link
2. Schedule recurring check-ins (monthly/quarterly)
3. Track candidate flow
4. Provide feedback on candidates
5. Share success stories

## Template Partnership Agreement

### Informal Partnership (Email Exchange)

> "We're happy to share apprenticeship opportunities with your students/graduates. You can direct interested candidates to our intake form: {LINK}. We'll keep you updated on placements and happy to provide feedback on candidates."

### Formal Partnership (MOU)

Key clauses:
1. Scope of collaboration
2. Candidate referral process
3. Feedback commitments
4. Data sharing (GDPR compliant)
5. Review frequency
6. Termination terms
